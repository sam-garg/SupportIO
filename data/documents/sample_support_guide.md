# Product Support & FAQ Guide

## 1. Authentication & Password Reset
To reset your account password:
1. Navigate to Settings > Security.
2. Click on "Reset Password".
3. Enter your current password followed by your new password twice.
4. If you forgot your current password, click "Forgot Password" on the login screen to receive a verification link via email.

## 2. Billing & Subscription Inquiries
- We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers.
- Invoices are generated automatically on the 1st of every calendar month.
- If a payment fails, the system retries automatically after 48 hours. If the payment continues to fail, contact billing support.
- Refunds can be requested within 14 days of purchase under our standard refund policy.

## 3. Product Installation & System Requirements
- SupportIO requires Python 3.10+ and a standard modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
- To launch locally, run: `streamlit run app.py`.
